Scope: whole web frontend (storefront Persuade/Operate, account and checkout Operate, admin Operate). Visitors: makers and students in India, mostly on phones; store admin on desktop and phone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Zetra is a maker's bench seen through glass. Every surface is a liquid-glass pane floating over a slow, luminous Zetra-blue field. Refuses the category default of a white grid of identical tiles on a flat page.

OWN-WORLD: Ambient field of drifting blue, cyan and faint ember light with grain; three glass tiers (thin for chrome, regular for cards, thick near-opaque for forms, tables, summaries); 1px specular edge, inner highlight, soft offset shadow. Palette: logo blue #1653A6 (dark #6AA5FF), logo red #F03C36 only for the Loupe ring, stock warnings, destructive actions. Hanken Grotesk for UI and display, JetBrains Mono only for data. Light and dark themes.

STORY: A maker lands, sees real parts on the bench and a working search, finds the part, reads its chip markings through the Loupe and its specs, adds to cart and pays with Razorpay, then tracks the order and downloads the GST invoice. Admin runs the store from a calmer glass shell on any device.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: a large glass stage; left two-thirds holds the headline "Parts for what you're building next." at 56–88px, one-sentence promise, the hero search combobox as the primary action, a glass "Browse all parts" button and three category links; right third stacks the three newest real parts as tilted thick-glass part tags (photo, name, price, stock), with the admin hero image above them when set; a promise row (genuine, shipping, datasheets, GST invoice) closes the stage. Floating glass pill navbar on top; on phones a glass bottom tab bar with a sliding droplet.

FORM: User-pinned direction; concept-seed not run because the user pinned the world in their own words: "We can implement liquid glass-like Apple stuff inside our frontend using impeccable", then chose "Glass everywhere" when asked how much glass the storefront should carry. Seed key: none (pinned). Signature interaction: the Loupe, a red-ringed glass magnifier over product photos (hover on desktop, Magnify toggle on touch). Motion grammar: glass droplets slide between active options; panes lift on hover; rise-in once on the hero; reduced motion and reduced transparency honoured.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
